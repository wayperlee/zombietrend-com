# ZombieTrend

Turn two photos into a 15-second AI [zombie hug / zombie trend video](https://zombietrend.com/) — couples, friends, or pets.

Homepage: https://zombietrend.com/

## What it does

Upload two photos, pick portrait or landscape, and choose standard 480p or HD 720p. Sign in, spend credits, and the finished video is emailed within about 12 hours. Pet & me stories are supported. Credits are one-time packs with no subscription.

Free prompt/photo guides are on the blog.

- [ZombieTrend](https://zombietrend.com/)
- [Pricing](https://zombietrend.com/pricing)

JPG, PNG, or WebP up to 8 MB. Online generation is still in development; delivery is by email for now.
